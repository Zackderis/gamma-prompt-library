# Gamma Design Skill - Prompt Library (52 prompts, 7 categories)

The assistant looks up the exact prompt here after the user picks one in the panel.


## 1. Business Strategy (8 prompts)

### 1.1 Business Model Visual Map
Based on everything you know about me, create a clear visual map of my business model showing how my business operates end-to-end. Include all key components such as how I attract leads, convert them into clients, deliver my [service offering], and generate revenue. Structure this as a flow diagram that shows how each part connects, including tools, channels, and actions. Highlight any gaps or inefficiencies visually. If needed, factor in [industry] and [primary offer].

### 1.2 Offer Stack & Value Ladder Diagram
Using everything you know about my business, design a visual value ladder showing all my offers from entry-level to premium. Include each offer's purpose, pricing tier, and how a client moves from one level to the next. Structure this as a clean, easy-to-understand diagram that shows progression and monetization flow. Highlight where I may be missing logical steps or upsell opportunities. If needed, factor in [current offers] and [target audience].

### 1.3 Ideal Customer Profile Snapshot Deck
Based on everything you know about my business, create a visually structured customer profile snapshot that clearly defines my ideal client. Include key sections such as demographics, psychographics, goals, pain points, objections, and buying triggers. Present this as a clean, slide-style layout that is easy to scan and understand. Ensure it reflects the type of client I want to attract, not just who I currently have. If needed, refine using [target market] and [core problem you solve].

### 1.4 Market Positioning Map
Using everything you know about my business and niche, create a visual positioning map that shows where I sit relative to competitors. Compare based on factors such as price, specialization, perceived value, and target audience. Structure this as a 2-axis or quadrant-style visual that makes my position clear. Then highlight where I can reposition to stand out more effectively. If needed, factor in [industry] and [key competitors or alternatives].

### 1.5 90-Day Business Roadmap Timeline
Based on everything you know about my current situation, create a visual 90-day roadmap that outlines what I should focus on each week to grow my business. Break this into phases with clear priorities across marketing, sales, and delivery. Structure it as a timeline that is easy to follow and act on. Focus only on high-impact actions. If needed, factor in [current monthly revenue] and [primary goal for next 90 days].

### 1.6 Revenue Streams Breakdown Dashboard
Using everything you know about my business, create a visual dashboard that breaks down all my current and potential revenue streams. Show how each stream contributes to total revenue, how scalable it is, and where there are opportunities for growth. Structure this in a way that is easy to understand at a glance, using categories or segments. If needed, factor in [current revenue sources] and [target income goal].

### 1.7 Business Bottleneck & Opportunity Map
Based on everything you know about my business, create a visual map showing the key bottlenecks currently limiting my growth and the opportunities available to overcome them. Structure this as a diagram linking problems to root causes and potential solutions. Highlight which bottlenecks have the highest impact if solved. Keep it clear and actionable. If needed, factor in [team size], [current workflow], and [growth challenges].

### 1.8 Scale vs Simplify Decision Framework
